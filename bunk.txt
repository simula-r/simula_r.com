Ok so here is the most useful rules of thumb / things to do for the gym/weight loss... I have been going to the gym off and on for like 10 years now lol. Luckily rn I got the time so I am properly back into it.

1. Diet is probably 80% of it. The gym is 20%. If your Diet is wrong you won't see a huge difference. But Diet doesn't need to be hard/scary.
2. Consistency is the most important thing. Whatever you end up doing it should be a daily weekly monthly practice. Most people go wrong because they go hard for 3 weeks and then burn out.
3. Girls usually shy away from lifting weights because they think it will make them bulky. It won't. It is actually a weight loss tool, and it just makes your natural shape more solid... in a good way. (Yes some girls take it too far/do body building but that is different)
4. After Diet cardio is the most important. Most people hate it. The goal is to just find something you actually like doing. For me its pickleball. For you maybe its swimming, treadmill with an audiobook, etc. But it has to be something you can easily do, ideally everyday... and it should be FUN! 


Ok so here it is, cook all your food. Your meals should be high protein. That is the most important thing. Ideally 1 gram of protein per body lb. Try to minimize carbs. You can still eat them but they should be things like desert/sweets and not main courses/bread, pasta, etc. So meat and veggies is the way to go. Just start eating mainly that. Then once a week you can cheat and get something that is high carb like ice cream or something you crave. Keep the cheat minimal tho, like yes cheat but don't over do it. The goal with diet is what can I do that I like for years to come. If you do this, after 3 months you will look very different. Even 1 month at your size/age probably 2 weeks you will see a change.

Then, start excercising daily. Ideally lifting weights for 20-30 minutes 2-3x a week, and then doing some form of cardio for 1hr everyday. If you find something you really have fun with you might end up doing cardio for hours! I play pickelball for like 4 hrs at a time some days. For lifting weights do 1 excercise per body part. Arms, back, abs, legs... and glutes (0_o) Thats it. You can look up "full body split 2-3x a week weight lifting for women" or I can give you something simple to follow.

That is really it. It does not need to be more complicated if your goal is losing weight and really just looking and feeling healthy. The fun part is with the diet you can also save a lot of money and generally eat better foods that make you feel great instantly. I eat a lot of avacados which are cheap and are super foods. They make my skin look super healthy.
